| Fathom (arXiv 2609.17652) | Bit-plane quantized key scan with per-query read depth; host-resident index and KV | No GPU cache. Optional per-layer **index-bit** budget, greedy by error drop per bit, mixed results ("Flat is the default") | Sink + local inside k only |

### Interpretation

1. **Per-layer GPU cache sizing from miss curves still looks open.** Neighbours size other things per layer or head: recall intervals (ScoutAttention), selection budgets (Mosaic), index bits (Fathom). Fathom's greedy per-layer plan gave mixed results on its resource; ours held out at 23%.
2. **Resident-page attention is partly anticipated.** Combining a GPU-resident set with retrieved top-k is common (Mosaic, ScoutAttention, top-k attention with a recent window). Not found: using a replacement cache's contents (pages selected recently) as the extra set, and measuring what it buys. The claim has to be that narrower one.
3. **The 10× miss gap gets a new data point.** vLLM's DSA model hits 80–90% at 2 × top-k, like HiSparse's 87%; our page-level Quest hits 98.6%. *Hypothesis:* token-level selection churns far more than 16-token pages. This makes experiment A1 below the first priority: if token-level traces miss about 10×, the per-layer split acts on far more traffic in DSA-style systems.

## A1 built: page-level against token-level selection

- `trace/collect.py --token-k 2048` also logs, at every step and layer, the top 2,048 single tokens shared by all heads, two ways: by attention weight averaged over heads (`tok_prob`, an ideal indexer) and by scaled logit averaged over heads (`tok_logit`, closer to DSA-style index scores). The newest token always leads (its KV is written straight to the GPU). Page-level Quest rankings are logged in the same run, so all three kinds see the same text.
- `sim/run_a1.py` replays each kind through the Phase 3 simulator with the same budget in tokens: k = 128 pages or 2,048 tokens, B = 2k units (8 MiB of KV per layer either way), and reports policies, bytes per step, per-layer profiles and the per-layer split (oracle and held-out over the other prompts).
- `run_a1.sh`: prompts 1 (single-document QA), 6 (long dialogue, most misses) and 56 (structured data, fewest).
